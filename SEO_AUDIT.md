## 3. Off-Page SEO & Linkable Asset Plan

kWhKlar does NOT utilize artificial PBNs, comment spam, or fake backlinks. Instead, the portal relies on **high-utility, data-rich linkable assets**:

1. **Interactive Real-Time Calculators**:
   - Universal Watt-to-kWh and Electricity Cost Calculator (`/stromkosten-rechner`).
   - Direct kWh-to-Money quick converter (`/kwh-rechner`).
2. **Official Household Benchmark Comparison Matrices**:
   - 1 to 5+ person reference consumption tables based on official *Stromspiegel* & *BDEW* datasets.
3. **Appliance Energy Cost Database**:
   - 25 major household devices with exact standby, active cycle, and annual cost breakdowns.
4. **Editorial Transparency & Methodology**:
   - Fully open formulas and documented regulatory breakdowns for journalists and researchers (`/quellen-methodik`).
